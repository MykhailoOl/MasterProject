How IdeaSpec works

Three simple steps from a rough idea to a usable plan.

1

Describe your idea

Write what you want to build in normal words, the way you would tell a friend.

2

Answer focused questions

IdeaSpec asks only about the missing pieces: who uses it, what it must do, and what to leave out.

3

Download your SPEC

Get a clear file you can hand to Cursor so the coding assistant builds what you meant.

Topics you can cover

Each topic is explained in everyday language. Turn on only the extras you need.

What you want

The problem, the better result, and what success looks like.

Who will use it

Customers, staff, and people who need stronger control.

What it should do

The main tasks people must complete in the first version.

How people open it

Website, phone, or computer—and which devices matter.

Optional extras

Sign-in, outside tools, going live, and other details when you need them.

Clear name and summary

Finish with a product name and a short summary that matches your answers.
